My Stoke — Privacy Policy & SEBI Disclaimer

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. No SEBI Advisory / No Broking

My Stoke is strictly a personal portfolio tracking and market condition viewer. We are not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser, Research Analyst, or Stock Broker. We do not provide buy, sell, or hold tips.

2. Information We Collect

Users manually enter their purchased stock symbols, quantities, and buy prices. If you choose to sign in with your Mobile Number or Gmail ID, it is used solely to sync your personal portfolio across devices. We never collect demat passwords, broker PINs, or banking credentials.

3. Data Security & Deletion

Your data is never sold or shared with third-party advertisers. You may delete your holdings anytime inside the app or email joyayogendra2726@gmail.com for account deletion.
